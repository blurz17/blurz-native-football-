# Training Mode User Guide

This guide explains how to launch, navigate, and master all drills in the Training Mode subsystem.

---

## 1. Accessing Training Mode

### From Main Menu:
1. Launch the game to view the Main Menu.
2. Select the **Training Mode** card.
3. Choose your desired starting drill:
   - **Free Play**: Full stadium open practice with teammates.
   - **Free Kick**: Set-piece practice over the wall.
   - **Corner Kick**: Cross deliveries and box heading.
   - **Penalty Shootout**: 1v1 spot kick duel.

---

## 2. In-Game Controls & Drill Navigation

### 2.1 The Training Pause Overlay
Tap the **Pause** icon in the upper-right corner at any time to open the dedicated 6-card training overlay:

```
┌─────────────────┬─────────────────┬─────────────────┐
│   CHANGE DRILL  │  NEXT VARIATION │   RESET DRILL   │
│  Cycle drill 1-4│  Angle/Side     │  Re-spot ball   │
├─────────────────┼─────────────────┼─────────────────┤
│   CAMERA VIEW   │  MATCH SETTINGS │   EXIT TO MENU  │
│  Normal / Player│  Audio & Video  │  Quit Training  │
└─────────────────┴─────────────────┴─────────────────┘
```

1. **Change Drill**: Instantly switches drills without returning to the main menu.
2. **Next Variation**: Cycles drill positions (e.g., changes free kick distance or corner kick side).
3. **Reset Drill**: Instantly returns the ball to the spot and repositions all players.
4. **Camera View**: Switches between **Normal Broadcast Tele** and **3rd-Person Player Cam**.
5. **Match Settings**: Configure audio volume and graphic quality.
6. **Exit to Menu**: Returns to the main launcher.

## 3. Drill Strategies & Mechanics

### 3.1 Free Play (Midfield Kickoff)
- **Starting Position**: Center circle $(0, 0, 0.11)$ with a stationary ball at feet.
- **Teammates**: 10 teammates deployed across the pitch in full formation. Practice short passing combinations, through balls into open channels, and long cross-field switches.
- **Opponent AI**: Opponent outfielders are parked off the field. The goalkeeper guards the net but will not aggressively rush you down.
- **Keeper Saves**: If the goalkeeper catches or parries your shot, the drill auto-resets after 2 seconds.

### 3.2 Free Kicks (4 Variations)
- **Variation 0 (Center 24m)**: Directly in front of goal. Aim for the top corners over the 3-man wall.
- **Variation 1 (Left Angle 23m)**: Angled from the left. Best suited for right-footed curlers or crosses to the far post.
- **Variation 2 (Right Angle 23m)**: Angled from the right. Best suited for left-footed curlers.
- **Variation 3 (Long Range 32m)**: 32 meters out. Power-driven shots or chipped passes into runners.
- **Set-Piece Camera**: Uses a behind-the-kicker camera view with camera-relative analog controls (Up = Forward toward goal).

### 3.3 Corner Kicks
- **Left & Right Corners**: Press **Cross / Pass** to deliver high or low balls into the danger area.
- **Headers & Volleys**: Time your header by pressing **Shoot** while the ball is airborne.

### 3.4 Penalty Shootout
- **11-Meter Spot Kick**: Pure 1v1 against the goalkeeper.
- **Power & Direction**: Push the analog stick toward the side netting while holding the Shoot button to calibrate shot power.
